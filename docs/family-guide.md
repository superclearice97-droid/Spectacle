# Getting Spectacle on your phone

You'll get a message from the person who set up the family cameras. It has two links. That's all you need.

## 1. Install the app

1. Tap the **first link** in the message. It downloads **Spectacle.apk**.
2. Tap the download when it finishes (or find it in your **Downloads**).
3. If your phone says installing apps from this source isn't allowed, tap **Settings**, turn on
   **Allow from this source**, then go back.
4. Tap **Install**, then **Open**.

> Your phone may warn that the app isn't from the Play Store. That's expected: Spectacle is shared
> privately within your family instead of through a store.

## 2. Connect

1. Go back to the message, **press and hold** it, and choose **Copy**.
2. In Spectacle, tap the big **Paste my invite** button.
3. Choose your name, a username and a password (at least 10 letters), tick the two boxes, and tap **Create account**.

## 3. Allow alerts

- When asked, tap **Allow** for notifications.
- When Spectacle asks to keep working in the background, tap **Continue**, then **Allow**.
  This makes sure motion alerts arrive straight away.

That's it. Spectacle starts by itself when your phone restarts.

## Turning an old phone into a camera

You'll get a **camera setup** message instead. Do the same steps 1 and 2 on the old phone. Then tick the box,
tap **Start camera** and allow the camera. Keep it plugged in. "Dim screen" turns the screen dark while it keeps
recording.

## Text size and screen readers

Spectacle follows your phone's text size (Settings → Display → Font size) and works with TalkBack.

## Updating

When there's a new version, the person who invited you will send a new download link. Install it the same way;
you stay signed in.
