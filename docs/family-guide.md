# Getting Spectacle on your phone

You'll get a message from the person who set up the family cameras. Just follow it from top to bottom.
It asks you to install two apps: **Tailscale** (a private connection to the family's cameras) and **Spectacle**.

## 1. Install Tailscale

1. Tap the **Tailscale** link in the message. It opens the Play Store. Tap **Install**, then **Open**.
2. Tap **Sign in** and choose your Google account (or another way to sign in). When Android asks
   to allow a VPN connection, tap **OK**. That's how Tailscale keeps the connection private.
3. Go back to the message and tap the **"connect to our cameras"** link, then **Accept**.

Leave Tailscale switched on. It uses very little battery.

## 2. Install Spectacle

1. Tap the **Install Spectacle** link in the message. It downloads **Spectacle.apk**.
2. Tap the download when it finishes (or find it in your **Downloads**).
3. If your phone says installing apps from this source isn't allowed, tap **Settings**, turn on
   **Allow from this source**, then go back.
4. Tap **Install**, then **Open**.

> Your phone may warn that the app isn't from the Play Store. That's expected: Spectacle is shared
> privately within your family instead of through a store.

## 3. Connect

1. Go back to the message, **press and hold** it, and choose **Copy**.
2. In Spectacle, tap the big **Paste my invite** button.
3. Choose your name, a username and a password (at least 10 letters), tick the two boxes, and tap **Create account**.

## 4. Allow alerts

- When asked, tap **Allow** for notifications.
- When Spectacle asks to keep working in the background, tap **Continue**, then **Allow**.
  This makes sure motion alerts arrive straight away.

That's it. Spectacle starts by itself when your phone restarts.

## Answering the doorbell and talking to visitors

When someone presses **Ring** on a door camera, your phone rings with **Someone is at Front door**.

1. Tap the notification (or **Answer**). You'll see them and hear them straight away.
2. Tap the big **Start talking** button when you want them to hear you. Tap **Stop talking** to listen again.
3. Tap **Hang up** when you're done.

The visitor hears a chime and sees that someone from the house is talking. Calls are never recorded.
You can also start a call yourself from any camera that has talking turned on: open the camera and tap **Start a call**.
The first time, your phone asks to use the microphone. Tap **Allow**.

## If it says "Can't reach home"

Tailscale is probably switched off. Tap **Open Tailscale**, switch it on so it says **Connected**,
then come back to Spectacle. It tries again by itself.

## Turning an old phone into a camera

You'll get a **camera setup** message instead. On the old phone, install Tailscale (signed in to the same account as the server) and Spectacle, then do step 3. Then tick the box,
tap **Start camera** and allow the camera (and the microphone, if the family uses two-way talk at that camera). Keep it plugged in. "Dim screen" turns the screen dark while it keeps
recording.

## Text size and screen readers

Spectacle follows your phone's text size (Settings → Display → Font size) and works with TalkBack.

## Updating

When there's a new version, the person who invited you will send a new download link. Install it the same way;
you stay signed in.
