# Camera notice templates

Print or copy these and adjust. They are examples, not legal advice.

## Sign for your door or gate

> **Cameras in use.** Video is recorded at this property for security and is kept for [14] days.
> Contact: [your name / phone or email]

## Sign for a doorbell / intercom camera

> **Video doorbell with two-way talk.** Press the button on the screen to ring. Someone from the house may answer and talk with you through this device. Calls are not recorded. Video is recorded and kept for [14] days.

## Message to guests, babysitters, cleaners and carers

> Hi [name], just so you know, we have security cameras at [front door, garden, driveway, hallway]. There are none in bedrooms, bathrooms or [other private spaces]. Cameras record video only, with no sound. [The front door camera also works as an intercom: we can talk with people at the door, it plays a chime when we do, and calls aren't recorded.] Footage is kept for [14] days and is seen only by our household. If you have questions, ask me anytime.

## For short-term rentals (if you let part of your home)

Disclose every camera, and its location, in the listing and again at check-in. Turn off or unplug any camera that could see inside the rented space. Follow the platform's rules: many ban indoor cameras entirely.
