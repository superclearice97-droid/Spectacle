3. Offline species database (optional; downloaded by `npm run species-db`, not bundled)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
a) Species explanations — Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org), via the
   Wikimedia REST API. Text licensed CC BY-SA 4.0
   (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Each entry keeps the
   article title and URL; the app shows this attribution beside the text and
   writes it into each species folder's info.md. If you redistribute that
   text, you must keep the attribution and share it under the same licence.
b) Classification — GBIF Backbone Taxonomy (https://www.gbif.org), CC BY 4.0.
   Citation: GBIF Secretariat, GBIF Backbone Taxonomy, https://doi.org/10.15468/39omei
c) Reference photos (optional) — Wikimedia Commons. Only freely licensed
   images (CC / public domain) are kept. Each photo's author, licence and
   source page are stored with it and shown in the app.
