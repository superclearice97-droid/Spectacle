Privacy policy

Version 2026-10-10. This policy covers this self-hosted copy of Spectacle. The household member who installed it (the "administrator") is responsible for it and for the data it holds.

The short version

  • Spectacle runs on a computer in your home. Video, photos, accounts and logs are stored there, not on a cloud service run by the app's authors.
  • Bug, flower and bird identification happens on that computer. Your photos are never sent anywhere to be identified.
  • Family members can see who is online and which camera they're watching.
  • Recordings are deleted automatically after the retention period (14 days by default).

What is collected

  • Video from cameras, recorded continuously (24/7) when recording is on. Audio is off by default and is only recorded if the administrator turns it on for an IP camera.
  • Motion events: the time, camera, and a snapshot image.
  • Biome log: photos cropped from camera frames or uploaded by you, the species the app thinks it shows, the time and camera.
  • Accounts: name, username, a scrypt hash of your password (never the password itself), your notification settings, the date you accepted these terms, and sign-in sessions.
  • Presence: while you have the app open, whether you're online, roughly which page you're on, which cameras you're viewing, and whether you're on a phone or computer. This is shown live to other signed-in family members and is not stored.
  • Push subscriptions: if you turn on phone notifications, your browser gives the server a delivery address at your browser maker's push service.
  • Family notes sent from the Family page are delivered live and not stored.

The Android app

  • The Spectacle app talks only to your family's own Spectacle server. It contains no analytics, ads, crash reporting or trackers, and doesn't use Google's push service.
  • Alerts: to deliver alerts with the app closed, it keeps one connection to your server and shows a small "Watching for alerts" notification (you can hide it in Android's notification settings). Turn alerts off any time in Settings.
  • Camera: used only when the phone is set up as a camera and you tap Start. The microphone is never used.
  • Stored on the phone: your server's address, its certificate fingerprint (so the app trusts only your server), your sign-in cookie and app settings. Android backups of the app are disabled. "Disconnect this phone" in Settings erases them.
  • Other permissions: notifications (to show alerts), start at boot (so alerts resume after a restart), and an optional request to ignore battery optimisation (so alerts aren't delayed).
  • Downloading the app from GitHub is subject to GitHub's privacy statement.

Who can see it

  • Every signed-in family member can watch all cameras, recordings, motion events and the biome log.
  • Administrators can add and remove cameras and members.
  • Anyone with physical or administrator access to the server computer can read everything stored on it.

What leaves your home

  • Push notifications are delivered through your browser maker's push service (for Chrome on Android, Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging; Firefox uses Mozilla's service). Message contents are end-to-end encrypted to your device; the service sees only timing and size. Notifications contain the camera name, time, and species names, never video or images.
  • ntfy (optional): if the administrator configures an ntfy address, motion alerts with the camera name and time go to that server.
  • Species database download (optional, off unless chosen): when the administrator runs npm run species-db, the server downloads public data from Wikipedia, Wikimedia Commons and GBIF. Only species names are sent; nothing about your home.
  • Remote access (Tailscale): to use Spectacle away from home, each member installs Tailscale and signs in with an account of their choice (for example Google). Connections between your phone and the home server are end-to-end encrypted (WireGuard); Tailscale cannot see your video or alerts. Tailscale does process account details (name, email), device names, IP addresses and connection metadata to set up the connection, under Tailscale's privacy policy. The administrator shares only the Spectacle server with you, not the rest of their network.

Spectacle contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

How long it's kept

  • Recordings and motion snapshots: the retention period set in config.json (default 14 days), or less if the disk runs low.
  • Biome photos and log: until deleted by the administrator (they are ordinary files in the data/biome folder).
  • Accounts: until deleted. Sessions expire after 30 days; invite codes after 7 days.

Your choices and rights

  • Download your data: Settings → Account → Download my data.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Account → Delete my account. Footage of the household is not personal to one account and is kept until it expires; ask the administrator if you want specific footage removed.
  • Notifications: turn them off or mute cameras in Settings at any time.
  • If you appear in footage and are not a member (for example a guest), contact the administrator to view or delete recordings of you. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or state privacy laws in the US), you may have a legal right to this.

Security

Passwords are hashed with scrypt; sessions use random tokens in HttpOnly, SameSite cookies; repeated wrong passwords are rate-limited; camera pairing links can be revoked by generating a new one. Use the HTTPS address (or Tailscale) so traffic is encrypted. Do not expose the server directly to the internet with port forwarding.

Children

Children may appear in household footage. Accounts are intended for adults and supervised teens the administrator chooses to invite.

Changes

If this policy changes in a new version of Spectacle, members are asked to review and accept it again the next time they sign in.

Contact

Questions about this installation go to the household administrator.
