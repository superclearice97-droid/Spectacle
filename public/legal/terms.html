1. What Spectacle is

Spectacle is free, self-hosted software for watching your own cameras, getting motion alerts and keeping a log of the plants and animals around your home. It is provided under the MIT License (see notices). The person who installs it runs it for their household.

2. Not a professional security or alarm system

Spectacle is not a monitored alarm, is not a replacement for one, and must not be relied on to protect people or property or to summon emergency help. Alerts can be late, missed or false: cameras go offline, Wi-Fi drops, phones silence notifications, power fails, disks fill up, and motion detection misses things and triggers on shadows, rain, pets and insects. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

3. Species identification is a guess

Identifications are made automatically by image-recognition models that can be wrong, and they only know a limited list of mostly North American species. Never eat, touch, handle, treat, or make health, safety, pest-control or legal decisions based on an identification from this app. Some look-alike plants and insects are poisonous, venomous or protected. Confirm with a qualified expert.

4. Your responsibilities when recording

  • Only install cameras where you have the right to record, and follow the laws where you live. See Camera & recording laws.
  • Do not record places where people expect privacy (bathrooms, changing areas, other people's bedrooms without consent), and do not aim cameras into neighbours' homes or private yards.
  • Tell household members, guests, renters and workers (such as nannies or cleaners) that cameras are recording. Posting a visible notice is recommended and in some places required.
  • Audio recording is off by default. Turning it on may require the consent of everyone recorded.
  • Don't use Spectacle to stalk, harass, or secretly monitor any person, including a partner or family member.
  • Keep your password private and remove members who should no longer have access.

5. Family members

By creating an account you agree that other members can see when you're online and which camera you're watching, and that you will treat footage of others respectfully and not share it outside the household without their permission, except with authorities when legally appropriate.
