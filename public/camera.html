Camera mode

This device will stream video to your family's Spectacle server, where it is recorded and checked for motion and wildlife.

Before starting:

  • Point it only at places you're allowed to record. Never at bathrooms, other people's bedrooms, or into neighbours' homes or yards.
  • Tell the people who live with you (and guests) that this camera records. In many places a visible "camera in use" sign is required or wise.
  • Only video is sent. The microphone is never used.
  • Keep this page open and the phone plugged in. Browsers stop the camera if you switch apps or lock the screen with this page hidden.
