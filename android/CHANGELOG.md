# Spectacle Android app — changes

## 1.2.0
- Two-way talk: answer the doorbell and talk to whoever is at a camera. Calls are live, announced on the camera, never recorded.
- Doorbell notifications ring on their own channel with an Answer button.
- Phone cameras can show a big Ring button for visitors.
- Smart detection alerts ("Person in Driveway") from servers and desktops that have it on.

## 1.0.0
- First release: one-tap setup from an invite message, live cameras, motion and new-species alerts straight from your
  home server (no Google push), phone-as-camera mode, friendly "Can't reach home" help for Tailscale,
  large text/TalkBack support.
